🗳️ General Voting Rules

  • Each student is entitled to one unique voting token.
  • Tokens are strictly single-use and cannot be shared or reused.
  • Attempts to vote multiple times or bypass system security will result in disqualification.
  • Voters must select exactly one candidate for President and one for Vice President.

⚖️ Integrity & Conduct

  • All candidates and supporters must maintain professional conduct throughout the election.
  • No campaigning or interference is allowed at the physical voting stations.
  • System logs are recorded to ensure transparency and auditability.

⚙️ System Usage

  • Ensure you have a stable network connection .
  • Follow the on-screen instructions carefully during the validation process.
  • If a token fails to validate, please report immediately to the election observer.
